Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does the process work?

It’s simple, with no surprises:

  1. First conversation: a 30-minute call to understand your business and what you need.
  2. Proposal: you get the scope, timeline and fixed price in writing.
  3. Design: I present the website design and we refine it together until it’s right.
  4. Build and launch: I build the website, test it on every device and publish it.
  5. Handover: you get the logins and a short training session to edit your content.
How long does it take to build a new website?

The free homepage redesign is ready in 2 days. The New Look takes about 1 week. For the Enquiry Engine, the timeline depends on how complex the project is and is set out in the proposal.

What if I don’t like the result?

We don’t move on to building until you’ve approved the design. Two rounds of revisions are included to adjust whatever you need, and we stay in touch throughout, so there are no surprises at the end. If you’d like to start with no commitment, the free homepage redesign shows you my work before you invest.

What do I need to prepare?

Very little. To start, all you need is your current website link, if you have one, an idea of what you want to achieve and a few websites you like, as a reference. During the project I’ll ask you for the text and photos you’d like to use and access to your domain to publish the website. If you don’t have everything ready, that’s fine: we’ll work out what’s missing together.

Do you work with businesses anywhere?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses all over Portugal and abroad. The whole process runs over video calls, email and messages, in English or Portuguese.

Can I update the website myself once it’s done?

Yes, in the areas that need frequent updates, such as the blog, events, projects or news. You get a simple dashboard to edit them without depending on anyone. For everything else, like changes to the core content, new images or design tweaks, I’m here to help.

Will my website show up on Google, ChatGPT and other AI tools?

I prepare the website for it. For Google: a fast, mobile-first website with well-defined titles, descriptions and structured data, and content that answers what your clients search for. For ChatGPT and other AI tools: clear information about what you do, for whom and where, direct answers to your clients’ questions and open access for these tools’ crawlers. Nobody can guarantee the top spot or a recommendation, but these are the factors that matter most. I explain it all in this article: how to get into ChatGPT’s answers.

What happens after the website goes live?

The website is yours, and you can manage it yourself. Even so, I recommend keeping it up to date, with content, security and performance in good shape, because that’s what keeps it working for your business. If you’d like help, I’m available to support you, with options tailored case by case and no obligation.
