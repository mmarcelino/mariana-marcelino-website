Questions

Frequently asked questions

What problems do you help solve?

The most common ones are:

  1. An outdated website: it doesn’t reflect the quality of your work and puts clients off before they get to know you.
  2. Visitors but no enquiries: people land on the website but leave without asking for a quote or booking.
  3. Low visibility: your business doesn’t show up on Google or in answers from ChatGPT and other AI tools.
  4. Manual work: time lost answering the same questions and handling requests and bookings by hand.
  5. No data: not knowing where clients come from or what’s working.
  6. No website of your own: relying only on social media or third-party platforms.
What kind of businesses do you work with?

I work with businesses in every sector, but I mostly help those that rely on enquiries and bookings and where each new client is worth a lot: clinics (dental, aesthetic, physiotherapy, psychology), law firms, accountants, holiday rentals, restaurants, estate agents, gyms and studios, architects and building and renovation companies. In these businesses, clients research and compare before choosing, so a website that builds trust and makes it easy to get in touch has a direct impact on revenue. Often, just a few new clients are enough for the website to pay for itself.

How much does a professional website cost?

It depends on what the website needs to do for your business. I work with fixed, transparent prices:

  1. Homepage redesign, free: to see my work on your own website, with no commitment.
  2. Fresh Look, 750€: a new design keeping your current structure and content, for websites of up to 4 pages.
  3. Enquiry Engine, from 1 800€: a custom website built to generate enquiries, with optimisation for Google and AI, forms, automation and metrics.

The final price is fixed in the proposal before we start. I explain what drives the price in this article: how much a professional website costs.

Will my website show up on Google, ChatGPT and other AI tools?

I prepare the website for it. For Google: a fast, mobile-first website with well-defined titles, descriptions and structured data, and content that answers what your clients search for. For ChatGPT and other AI tools: clear information about what you do, for whom and where, direct answers to your clients’ questions and open access for these tools’ crawlers. Nobody can guarantee the top spot or a recommendation, but these are the factors that matter most. I explain it all in this article: how to get into ChatGPT’s answers.

How does the process work?

It’s simple, with no surprises:

  1. Intro call: a 30-minute call to understand your business and what you need.
  2. Proposal: you get the scope, timeline and fixed price in writing.
  3. Design: I present the website design and we refine it together until it’s right.
  4. Build and launch: I build the website, test it on every device and publish it.
  5. Handover: you get the logins and a short training session to edit your content.
How long does it take to build a new website?

The free homepage redesign is ready in 2 days. Fresh Look takes about a week. For the Enquiry Engine, the timeline depends on how complex the project is and is set out in the proposal.

What if I don’t like the result?

Nothing gets built until you’ve approved the design. Two rounds of revisions are included to adjust whatever you need, and we stay in touch throughout, so there are no surprises at the end. If you’d like to start with no commitment, the free homepage redesign shows you my work before you invest.

What do I need to prepare?

Very little. To start, all I need is a link to your current website (if you have one), an idea of what you want to achieve and a few websites you like for reference. During the project I’ll ask you for the text and photos you’d like to use and access to your domain to publish the website. If you don’t have everything ready, that’s fine: we’ll work out what’s missing together.

Do you only work in Lisbon, or with clients elsewhere?

I’m based in Lisbon but work remotely with businesses all over Portugal, from Porto to the Algarve and the islands, and abroad. The whole process runs over video calls, email and messages, in English or Portuguese. If you’re in the Lisbon area, we can also meet in person.

Once it’s live, can I update the website myself?

Yes. The website is yours, and you can manage it yourself. For the areas that need frequent updates, such as the blog, events, projects or news, you get a simple dashboard to edit them without depending on anyone. For everything else, like changes to the core content, new images or design tweaks, I’m here to help. I recommend keeping it up to date, with content, security and performance in good shape, because that’s what keeps it working for your business. If you’d like, I can support you, with options tailored to you and no obligation.
