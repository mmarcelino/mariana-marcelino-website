Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect through this website, what I use it for, who I share it with and what rights you have over it, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

Mariana Marcelino is the controller of the data collected on this website. For any privacy question, write to info@mariana-marcelino.com.

What data I collect

  • Free redesign request: your email address and your website link, which you enter in the homepage forms.
  • Free guide request: your email address and your website link, which you enter to download the guide “Eight signs your website is driving clients away”.
  • Contact form and email: your name (optional), your email address and whatever information you choose to share in your message.
  • Booking a call: when you book a call, you are redirected to Calendly, where you enter your name, email and any other information the form asks for.
  • Technical data: as with any website, your browser automatically sends your IP address and technical information to the hosting server and to the services that load resources on the page, such as fonts.

I don’t collect special categories of data, and this website is not intended for children under 16.

What I use your data for

  • To reply to your request, prepare the free redesign and the proposal you asked for, and provide the free guide. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To provide the services you hire and to meet legal obligations, such as invoicing. Legal basis: performance of a contract and compliance with a legal obligation.
  • To keep the website working and secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

I don’t use your data for advertising and I don’t sell it to third parties.

Who I share your data with

For the website to work, I rely on service providers that process data on my behalf or that your browser connects to:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): receives the data from the website’s forms (free redesign, free guide and contact: name, email, website link, message and page language) and forwards it to my email. No account is needed and the data isn’t used for any other purpose.
  • Calendly (calendly.com): handles call bookings. The data you enter on the booking page is processed by Calendly, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (Google): provides the website’s typeface. When the page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Email and website hosting providers: store the messages I receive and serve the website’s pages.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, particularly in the United States. In those cases, transfers are made with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep your data

  • Free redesign requests and enquiries that don’t lead to work: up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client data: for the duration of the contract and, after that, for as long as the law requires, for example for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can, at any time, ask to access your data, correct or delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Just write to info@mariana-marcelino.com. I reply within one month at the latest.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

Cookies

This website doesn’t use its own cookies. You can read the details in the Cookie Policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when new services are added to the website. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page.
