Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect through this website, what I use it for, who I share it with and what rights you have over it, under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

Mariana Marcelino is the controller of the data collected on this website. For any privacy question, write to info@mariana-marcelino.com.

What data I collect

  • Free redesign request: your email address and your website link, which you enter in the homepage forms.
  • Website diagnosis: your answers to the free diagnosis, your website address (if you give it) and, if you ask for the full report, your email address.
  • Contact form and email: your name (optional), your email address and whatever information you choose to share in your message.
  • Chat: if you use the website’s chat assistant, the messages you write. The conversation is only kept in your browser while the tab is open. If you choose “Email this conversation”, I receive the conversation and the email you enter.
  • Booking a call: when you click “Book a call”, Calendly’s calendar opens in a window on this website, where you enter your name, email and any other information the booking form asks for.
  • Technical data: as with any website, your browser automatically sends your IP address and technical information to the hosting server and, when you use them, to the third-party services built into the page, such as the booking calendar.

I don’t collect special categories of data, and this website is not intended for children under 16.

What I use your data for

  • To reply to your request, prepare the free redesign and the proposal you asked for, and send the diagnosis report. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To provide the services you hire and to meet legal obligations, such as invoicing. Legal basis: performance of a contract and compliance with a legal obligation.
  • To keep the website working and secure, and to measure visits anonymously so it can be improved. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

I don’t use your data for advertising and I don’t sell it to third parties.

Who I share your data with

For the website to work, I rely on service providers that process data on my behalf or that your browser connects to:

  • Resend (resend.com): sends the emails generated by the website’s forms (free redesign, diagnosis and contact: name, email, website link, message and page language), that is, the notification I receive and the confirmation sent to you. The data isn’t used for any other purpose.
  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com): a backup service that delivers the forms by email if the one above isn’t available. The data isn’t used for any other purpose.
  • Vercel Web Analytics (vercel.com): measures visits to the website in an aggregated, anonymous way (pages viewed, where the visit came from, country, device type and browser). It doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t store your IP address and can’t identify you or follow you across websites.
  • Anthropic (anthropic.com): generates the replies of the website’s chat assistant, using the Claude AI model. It only receives the messages in the conversation, to reply, and doesn’t use them to train its models. The chat doesn’t ask for or store personal data unless you choose to email the conversation.
  • Calendly (calendly.com): handles call bookings. The calendar only loads when you click “Book a call”; at that point, your browser connects to Calendly’s servers, which receive your IP address and may set cookies. The data you enter in the calendar is processed by Calendly, under its own privacy policy.
  • Email and website hosting providers: store the messages I receive and serve the website’s pages.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, particularly in the United States. In those cases, transfers are made with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep your data

  • Free redesign requests and enquiries that don’t lead to work: up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client data: for the duration of the contract and, after that, for as long as the law requires, for example for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can, at any time, ask to access your data, correct or delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Just write to info@mariana-marcelino.com. I reply within one month at the latest.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).

Cookies

This website doesn’t use its own cookies. Calendly may set cookies when you open the booking calendar. You can read the details in the Cookie Policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when new services are added to the website. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page.
